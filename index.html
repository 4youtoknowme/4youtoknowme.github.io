I run and I film. Full and half marathons, road and mountain, usually with a camera in my hand for the whole thing.

Most race footage is shot from behind the barriers, so it only ever sees the front of the field. Mine is shot from inside the pack, at the pace of the people actually running it, which is where the race is.

Based in Delhi. I travel for races anywhere. Most recently the Surya Spiti Challenge, filmed on foot at altitude across the valley.
